LLMs as a Tool Not a Crutch

LLMs (ChatGPT) can be useful in unpacking complex concepts and assisting students with organising their thoughts. However, they are a tool to aid learning and not a replacement for student effort.

Overuse of LLMs can inhibit the development of student skills. Using an LLM to complete part of an assignment may enable a student to complete the assignment but does not promote understanding. Errors can also occur with LLMs so users should exercise critical thinking and verify information as necessary.

LLMs can be used in practice, feedback and explanation while students are still engaged in the learning process. This ensures that LLMs are used as a tool in education and not a crutch.
